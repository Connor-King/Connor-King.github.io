Soul Splicers

My independent studio's dark fantasy action roguelike, built solo from scratch in Unity/C#. The alpha is playable in the browser.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Founder
  • Live alpha

Soul Splicers is my independent studio, and its first game shares the name: a 2D dark fantasy action roguelike you can play in your browser. Players harvest souls, splice their Experiment into something stronger, and conquer the keep. The alpha is live at soulsplicers.com.

I'm the founder and solo systems engineer:

  • Architected and developed a full commercial 2D action-platformer from scratch in Unity/C#, designing all core state machines, combat mechanics, dynamic physics, and custom editor tools independently.
  • Engineered custom Web3/API backend integrations, player analytics telemetry, and web services powering live pre-alpha community deployments.
  • Managed full product lifecycle including CI/CD build pipelines, performance profiling across hardware configurations, and active player feedback integration.

In the game, combat mixes melee weapons, a six-shooter and sorcery, and movement includes sliding, swinging and ground-pounding through the keep. Each run feeds the next: souls harvested from enemies are spent in the Soul Shop to level health, damage, magic and mana and unlock new abilities, and leaderboards track the most souls harvested and the highest round reached.
