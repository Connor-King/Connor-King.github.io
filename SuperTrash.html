Super Trash

A side-scrolling shoot 'em up built in one week from a set of provided, recycled assets.

  • Unity
  • State-machine AI
  • Game jam

Super Trash was made during a 1-week game jam at university, where our team of 4 had to make a game using assets provided to us. Looking through them, we found a flying animation for the main character, which sparked the idea of a side-scrolling shoot 'em up made with Unity.

The game has four levels and four boss fights. My role was to make the enemies and bosses, and I learnt a lot about state machines and how they can drive AI behaviour.
