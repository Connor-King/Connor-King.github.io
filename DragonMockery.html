Dragon Mockery (In Beta)

A rogue-like dungeon crawler with a procedurally generated dungeon and a dragon boss that attacks with insults.

  • Unity
  • Enemy AI
  • Procedural dungeon
  • Game jam

A game jam game based on the theme "Shooter game where you suffer the mockery of dragons in beta". It's a rogue-like dungeon crawler where you explore a procedurally generated dungeon, fighting enemies on your way to a dragon boss that uses insults as attacks.

The limited time (1 week) and the random theme made for an interesting combination. My role was to create the enemy AI, their attacks, and the animation controllers to go with them.
