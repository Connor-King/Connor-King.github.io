Sibling Sucker Punch

A 2D couch-multiplayer beat 'em up made in 48 hours for Global Game Jam 2019. 1st place on site at The Foundry.

  • Unity
  • Global Game Jam 2019
  • 1st place

Sibling Sucker Punch is a game my team made for the Global Game Jam at The Foundry over 48 hours. It's a competitive couch multiplayer beat 'em up where you have to beat up your brother without your mum finding out. The jam's theme was "What Home Means To You", which made us think about how we used to fight with our siblings.

There are two characters, the older brother and the younger brother. They're mechanically identical apart from their special attacks: the younger brother can pick up and throw a pokeball, while the older brother uses his gold chain as a whip. Punching the other player into the furniture breaks it, and if too much furniture gets broken, Mum comes in, stops the fight, and the player with the least health wins.

My role was to create the movement, fighting and special attacks. We won first place at our location. The game was made in Unity, and all scripts, art and sound were made on site.
