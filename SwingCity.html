Swing City

A physics-based 2.5D 1v1 claw game made in 48 hours for Global Game Jam 2020. 3rd place on site.

  • Unity
  • Physics
  • Global Game Jam 2020
  • 3rd place

Swing City is a game my team and I made at the Global Game Jam 2020 at The Foundry over 48 hours. The theme was "Repair". It's a physics-based 1v1 claw game where you compete with a friend to rebuild the highest pile of blocks while dealing with bombs and a menacing opponent.

My role was to create the main arm mechanics. This included implementing rope-style physics for the swinging and a claw that could pick up objects in the game. All scripts, art and sound were made on site during the jam.

Event

Result
3rd place on site
